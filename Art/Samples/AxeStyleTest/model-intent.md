# AxeStyleTest model intent

This source is authored from the accepted image-model Product Target at `Reference/target.png`.

Priority order:

1. Preserve the large double-blade silhouette.
2. Preserve the long brown handle with four distinct green wrap regions.
3. Preserve the dark rectangular central head/eye with a gold square front inset.
4. Preserve the gray square pommel and gray collar below the head.
5. Preserve stepped silver blade construction and bright outer cutting edges.
6. Keep the whole weapon one rigid `Axe` Part.

The Product Target remains authority. The VoxelSpec may change after proof comparison; the target must not be simplified to match this first model.
