# High-Resolution Staff Proof Visual Audit

## Verdict

PASS for the **high-resolution normalized-authoring solution proof**.

This is not approval to replace published Staff01 yet. The sample demonstrates that VPU64 provides enough authored resolution to move from primitive box construction toward the uploaded product-quality visual language.

## Comparison

Current Staff01:
- narrow low-VPU shaft dominates the silhouette;
- top ornament remains mostly cross-bars/caps;
- little multi-axis volume or curved stepped construction is available.

VPU64 proof:
- filled stepped cyan orb reads as a volumetric focal mass;
- purple cage follows curved rings in two planes rather than flat bars;
- gold structural nodes create readable layered depth;
- shaft uses multiple thicknesses, bends, collars, and material values;
- small floating accent cubes and asymmetric support geometry produce a more product-like silhouette.

## Remaining art-direction gap

The proof is materially closer to the uploaded reference, but it is still an engineering-quality sample rather than final production art. A replacement Staff01 should further tune asymmetry, exact cage proportions, palette/value hierarchy, and silhouette rhythm against the visual target before publication.
