# SpearStyleTest model intent

This is a non-published geometry/style sample under `Samples`. It does not claim target-driven v3 reference acceptance or catalog publication. Promote it only after the normal Product Target -> Composition -> Part Sheet -> density/source -> proof gates are completed.

Design goals:

1. Read immediately as a mobile-game spear with a long, simple shaft and oversized readable spearhead.
2. Use a broad leaf-shaped steel head rather than a needle point so the silhouette survives at small screen size.
3. Preserve a stepped center ridge and bright cutting-edge band on the spearhead.
4. Separate head, socket/collar, shaft, grip wraps, and butt cap with clear material blocks.
5. Keep the shaft warm brown, use three green grip-wrap bands near the lower-middle, and use restrained bronze accents at the collar and butt cap.
6. Keep the whole sample as one rigid `Spear` part with its long axis along +Z.

The sample uses VPU16. The ~42-voxel long axis gives a ~2.6-unit weapon, while the spearhead remains 7-9 voxels wide at its broadest point and supports intentional one-voxel edge/highlight bands without drifting into high-density smoothing.
