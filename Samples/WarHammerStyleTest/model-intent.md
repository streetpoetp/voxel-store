# WarHammerStyleTest model intent

This is a non-published geometry/style sample under `Samples`. It does not claim target-driven v3 reference acceptance or catalog publication. Promote it only after the normal Product Target -> Composition -> Part Sheet -> density/source -> proof gates are completed.

Design goals:

1. Read immediately as a compact one-handed war hammer at mobile-game scale.
2. Use an asymmetric head: a broad square striking face on +X and a short stepped rear spike on -X.
3. Keep the head visually heavy with dark gunmetal mass, brighter steel bevels, and one restrained bronze inset.
4. Keep the shaft simple and chunky: warm brown wood, three green grip-wrap bands, and a dark metal pommel.
5. Preserve clear silhouette breaks between pommel, grip, collar, central head, striking face, and rear spike.
6. Keep the whole sample as one rigid `WarHammer` part with the long axis along +Z.

The sample uses VPU16. Its ~28-voxel long axis produces a ~1.75-unit weapon while retaining intentional one-voxel bevel/highlight bands and a visibly stepped spike without drifting into smooth high-density modeling.
